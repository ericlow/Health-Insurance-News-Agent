Date 4/17/2026 10:00:17 AM Dear Ms. Tonkin:
The Department of Managed Health Care (the “Department”) has received
the information submitted in Block Transfer Filing 2026-PROV-97 (“Filing”)
submitted by Cigna HealthCare of California, Inc. (the “Plan”) on March 10,
2026. Based upon the representations within the Filing, the number of
enrollees who will be reassigned or redirected as a result of the contract
termination is less than 2,000. At this time, the Department accepts the
Filing as an Information Only Filing.
The Department requests that the Plan immediately notify the Department
regarding any changed circumstances, incorrect information, or new
information material to the Plan's submission regarding this matter. The
Department reserves its right to revoke or modify this acceptance of the
Plan's submission as an Information Only Filing, based upon changed
circumstances or based upon the Department's receipt of new or additional
information.
The Department requests that the Plan immediately notify the Department
regarding any update to the number of enrollees who have been authorized
or approved to receive procedures or services from providers within the
Terminating Medical Group within 180 days of the termination date.
Within one business day after the contract termination date, please advise
the Department of the contracting status: agreement reached, extension
(include new termination date), or not contracted. If the contracting status
is “not contracted,” please provide a courtesy copy of the enrollee notice
sent by the Plan regarding the contract termination.
Thank you for submitting this information regarding the potential contract
termination. Please contact me if you have any questions.
