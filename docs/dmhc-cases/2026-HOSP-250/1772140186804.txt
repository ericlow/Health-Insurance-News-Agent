Re: Cause of Late Enrollee Notice/ Request for Waiver of 60-Day Notice Requirement
Dear DMHC Representatives,
This letter serves as Health Net of California, Inc.’s (“Health Net” or the “Plan”) formal request for a waiver of the
60 day enrollee notice requirement and approval to provide 30 day advance notice in connection with the
provider contract termination of ADVENTIST HEALTH SIMI VALLEY (HOSP) and the associated block transfer filing
‑ ‑
#2026 HOSP 249.
‑ ‑
Pursuant to Health and Safety Code § 1373.65(b) and Title 28, California Code of Regulations § 1300.67.1.3(e),
notice to enrollees is generally required at least sixty (60) days prior to a provider contract termination. However,
the regulations allow the Plan to request a waiver of this requirement where good cause circumstances exist.
‑
Basis for Waiver Request
The Plan respectfully requests this waiver based on good cause circumstances, specifically:
‑
• Recent amendments to block transfer filing requirements, which have resulted in revised timelines and
sequencing of regulatory submissions; and
• A resulting timing conflict with regulatory review and approval processes, which has made compliance
with the 60 day enrollee notice requirement impracticable despite the Plan’s diligent and timely efforts.
‑
Due to these circumstances, the Plan is unable to issue enrollee notices sixty (60) days in advance of the contract
termination without creating inconsistencies between regulatory approvals, block transfer implementation, and
member communications.
Healthnet.com
Member Protection and Continuity of Care
The Plan remains committed to protecting enrollees and ensuring continuity of care. In support of this
commitment:
• Enrollees will receive clear and timely written notice at least thirty (30) days prior to the effective
termination date;
• Affected members will be provided with information regarding continuity of care rights, access to
alternative contracted providers, and assistance with provider selection as applicable; and
• The Plan will continue to comply with all other applicable statutory and regulatory requirements related
to network adequacy and continuity of care.
Request
Accordingly, the Plan respectfully requests that the Department approve a waiver of the 60 day notice
requirement and permit issuance of 30 day advance notice to enrollees for this termination.
‑
‑
If the Department requires any additional information or documentation to evaluate this request, the Plan will
promptly provide it.
