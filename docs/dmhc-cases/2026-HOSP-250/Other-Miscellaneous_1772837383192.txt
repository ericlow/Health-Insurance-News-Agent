In certain cases, you may be able to keep getting services from the hospital if it agrees and if the hospital has
been treating you for any Qualifying Conditions, listed below:
• Acute condition, for the time the condition lasts;
• Serious chronic (long-term) condition, for up to 12 months;
• Pregnancy, during the pregnancy and 6 months after giving birth;
• Maternal mental health condition, for up to 12 months;
• Terminal illness or conditions, may exceed 12 months from the contract termination date or 12
months from the effective date of coverage for a new enrollee;
• Children ages birth to 36 months, for up to 12 months;
• You have surgery or other procedures authorized by our plan as part of a documented course of
treatment.
