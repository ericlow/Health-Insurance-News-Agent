As you are aware, it is the intent and desire of UC Davis Medical Group (“PG” or “Group”) to renegotiate
the terms and conditions (including, but not limited to, the rates) of the CIGNA Healthcare of California, Inc.
(“CIGNA”) Capitated Physician Group Services Agreement (“Agreement”) dated November 1, 1999, as
subsequently amended, by and between CIGNA and Group through the University of California (“UC”)
system wide negotiation coordinated on behalf of the UC through the law offices of Rosenberg & Pick.
As you are further aware, Section 6.1, Continuation Term of the Agreement states:
Continuation Term. The continuation term of this Agreement (“Continuation Term”) shall become
effective as of July 1, 2023, and shall continue in effect through June 30, 2026, unless earlier
terminated as set forth in this Agreement. Unless either party notifies the other party at least one
hundred eighty (180) days prior to the expiration of the Continuation Term, this Agreement shall
automatically renew for additional terms of one (1) year each, unless and until terminated as set
forth in this Agreement.
In accordance with Section 6.1 “Continuation Term,” this letter shall serve as Group’s one hundred eighty
(180) calendar days’ prior written notice of Group’s intent to negotiate the renewal terms of the Agreement.
As required by Section 6.1 “Continuation Term” of the Agreement, this letter shall also serve to provide
notice that if the parties are unable to successfully negotiate and finalize the renewal terms of the
Agreement by June 30, 2026 the Agreement shall terminate effective at midnight on June 30, 2026, unless
otherwise mutually agreed to in writing by the parties.
