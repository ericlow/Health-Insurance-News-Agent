The Department of Managed Health Care (Department) has reviewed the information in the
above-referenced Block Transfer Filing (Filing) submitted by Cigna HealthCare of
California, Inc. (Plan) for compliance with the requirements of the Knox-Keene Health
Care Service Plan Act of 1975, as amended (Cal. Health & Safety Code § 1340 et seq.)
and the regulations promulgated thereunder (Cal. Code Regs., Tit. 28, § 1000 et seq.).
In the event of contract termination, the Filing proposes to remove Community Health
Partners (Terminating Provider Group) from the Plan’s provider network. The effective
date of contract termination between the Terminating Provider Group and the Plan
(Termination Date) is currently July 1, 2026.
The Plan proposes to redirect affected enrollees to the following Receiving Provider
Groups:
• Santé Community Physicians IPA Medical Corporation
Based on the Plan’s representations and information provided through the review
process, the Department hereby approves the Plan’s Filing, subject to the following:
1. The Plan shall provide written notice to each affected enrollee at least sixty (60)
calendar days prior to the Termination Date, subject to any waivers the
Department has granted the Plan for this Filing.

2. With respect to enrollees who are approved to receive Completion of Covered
Services from the Terminating Provider Group, the Plan shall ensure that the
copayment, deductible, percentage coinsurance or any other cost sharing
component for such care is no greater than the financial responsibility the
enrollee would have had if he or she received the services from the Terminating
Provider Group prior to the Termination Date. (See Cal. Health & Safety Code §
1373.96.).
3. If the Plan reaches an agreement with the Terminating Provider Group after the
above enrollee transfer notices are sent, the Plan must promptly inform the
Department and convey an additional enrollee notification to each affected
enrollee in accordance with the requirements set forth in Cal. Code Regs., Tit.
28, § 1300.67.1.3(f).
4. In the event that the effective date of removal of the Terminating Provider Group
from the Plan’s network is delayed by 90 or more days, this approval shall no
longer be effective, unless the Plan requests an extension of this Approval and
the Department grants the Plan’s request.
5. If the Plan determines that information submitted in this Filing is materially
incorrect, it will promptly notify the Department. The Plan must also promptly
notify the Department of any changed circumstances or new information that is
material to this Filing.
6. The Department reserves the right to revoke, modify or terminate this approval at
any time based upon changed circumstances or new information that is material
to the filing, or for non-compliance by the Plan with any of the Conditions listed
above.
