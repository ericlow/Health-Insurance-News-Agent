Re: UCSD Medical Group HMO – Notice of Renegotiation/Termination
To Whom It May Concern:
This letter (“Notice of Renegotiation/Termination”) shall serve to advise Cigna
Healthcareof California, Inc.(“CHC” or “Cigna”) that The Regents of the University of
California, a California Constitutional Corporation, on behalf ofUCSDMedical Group
(“Group”) desires to renegotiate the terms of that certain NineteenthAmendment to
the Capitated Physician Group Services Agreement, dated July1, 2023, as amended,
by and between CHC and Group (“Agreement”).
This Notice of Renegotiation/Termination is provided to CHC pursuant to Section 6.1
of the Agreement which provides in pertinent part as follows:
“Continuation Term. The continuation term of this Agreement (“Continuation Term”)
shall become effective as of July 1, 2023 and shall continue in effect through June 30,
2026, unless earlier terminated as set forth in this Agreement. Unless either party
notifies the other party at least one hundred eighty (180) days prior to the expiration
of the Continuation Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for additional
terms of one (1) year each, unless and until terminated as set forth in this Agreement.”
Although it is the intent and desire of Group to renegotiate the terms of the
Agreement, in the unlikely event the parties are unable to successfully renegotiate
this Agreement by June 30,2026, this letter shall serve as a termination notice with
respect to the Agreement, and the Agreement shall terminate effective July 1, 2026.
