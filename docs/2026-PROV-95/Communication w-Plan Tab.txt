By Lea Anna Tonkin Hi Nune, Please refer to the Plan's response to your inquiry below. a.
Date 4/16/2026 9:58:24 AM Identify any waivers or alternate access standards approved by the
Department for this area (including the filing number and approval date);
There are no waivers or alternate access standards approved by the
Department for this area. However, waivers or alternate access standards
are not needed. The Plan clarifies that it reassigned 38 enrollees to PCPs
that are located beyond the standard 15 mile distance requirement.
However, the drive time to these PCPs is approximately 30 minutes, which is
consistent with the applicable access standards. At the time the
reassignment review was conducted, these PCPs were the only providers
available to accept new patients. The Plan confirms that additional PCP
alternatives have since become available, allowing members to select PCPs
located within the 15 mile and 30 minute access standards. b. The total
number of enrollees specified by ZIP Code, if any, who have a residence or
workplace beyond 30 minutes or 15 miles of a contracting or plan-operated
hospital; The Plan confirms that there are no enrollees who have a residence
or workplace beyond 30 minutes or 15 miles of a contracting or plan-
operated hospital. All enrollees have access to a contracted or plan operated
hospital within 30 minutes or 15 miles. c. The Plan’s process for ensuring
compliance with Cal. Code Regs., Tit. 28, § 1300.51(d)(H)(ii). (ii) Hospitals.
In the case of a full-service plan, all enrollees have a residence or workplace
within 30 minutes or 15 miles of a contracting or plan-operated hospital
which has a capacity to serve the entire dependent enrollee population
based on normal utilization, and, if separate from such hospital, a
contracting or plan-operated provider of all emergency health care services.
The plan describes its process as follows: (1) Contracts with hospitals to
ensure adequate coverage throughout its service areas; (2) performs geo-
access analyses to confirm timely access; (3) confirms that contracted
hospitals have capacity to serve the dependent enrollee population based on
normal utilization; (4) identifies any measures it takes if access isn’t
available Let me know if you have any additional questions. Thank you, Lea
Anna Tonkin
