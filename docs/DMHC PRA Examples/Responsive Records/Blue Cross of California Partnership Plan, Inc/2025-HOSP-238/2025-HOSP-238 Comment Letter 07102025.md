# DMHC Comment Letter — Block Transfer 2025-HOSP-238

**Date:** July 10, 2025
**From:** Cindy Nguyen, Associate Governmental Program Analyst, Office of Plan Monitoring, DMHC
**To:** Evan Escobar, Blue Cross of California Partnership Plan, 1121 L Street Suite 500, Sacramento CA 95814
**Re:** Proposed Contract Termination with Lucile Salter Packard Children's Hospital at Stanford — Block Transfer Filing No. 2025-HOSP-238 (filed July 7, 2025)
**Response Due:** July 17, 2025 via Block Transfer portal as "Response to Comment Letter"

The Department is reviewing the above-referenced Block Transfer Filing for compliance with the Knox-Keene Health Care Service Plan Act of 1975 (Cal. Health & Safety Code § 1340 et seq.) and regulations thereunder (Cal. Code Regs., Tit. 28, § 1000 et seq.). The filing proposes to remove Lucile Salter Packard Children's Hospital at Stanford from the Plan's hospital network effective September 1, 2025.

## Comments and Requests

**1. Contract Scope**
Please confirm if the contract for Lucile Salter Packard Children's Hospital at Stanford is an individual contract or if it is part of the Stanford Health System (one contract) termination.

**2. Enrollee Transfer Notice — Missing Information**
Resubmit the Enrollee Transfer Notice to include:
- Statement informing enrollees that they may contact the Plan for language assistance, including contact information.

**3. LaSalle Medical Associates — Provider Group Information**
Please summarize or confirm:
- Name and contact information
- Summary of any concerns or issues raised by the provider group regarding the Block Transfer
- Whether the Plan has sent or received a notice of contract termination from the provider group, and if so, whether that contract terminates within the next 75 days

**4. LaSalle Medical Associates — Admitting Privileges at Alternate Hospitals**
Confirm the number of PCPs, Hospitalists, and Specialists who admit to each alternate hospital:
- Eden Medical Center
- El Camino Health
- Kindred Hospital — San Francisco Bay Area
- Regional Medical of San Jose

**5. LPCH Specialty Services**
Please indicate whether the Terminating Hospital is or provides any of the following:
- Tertiary care, quaternary care, cancer, burn, or other high-level specialty services
- Transplant services — if yes:
  - What types of transplants
  - Whether the contract termination includes the transplant contract
  - The termination date of the transplant contract if separate
- Recognition/designation as a Center of Excellence or similar (e.g., Bariatric Surgery, Spine Surgery) — if yes, identify the services

**6. Provider Group Ability to Redirect Enrollees**
For LaSalle Medical Associates:
- Is there any specialty for which all associated specialists have admitting privileges only at the Terminating Hospital and not at any Alternate Hospital?
  - If yes: identify the specialty and explain how enrollees will receive care post-termination
- If the provider group has no PCPs or specialists with admitting privileges at Alternate Hospitals, explain how enrollees will be admitted (e.g., via hospitalists, Plan admission process)
- If hospitalists are utilized: name of group, number of hospitalists, and whether they can admit for any medically necessary services

**7. LaSalle Service Contract with LPCH — Confirmation**
The Plan indicates LaSalle Medical Associates maintains a service contract with the Terminating Hospital. Please confirm that in the event of contract termination:
- The provider group shall continue to refer enrollees to the Terminating Hospital for all medically necessary services consistent with current referral patterns
- Plan enrollees will not be redirected and will be allowed continued access at current (pre-termination) in-network benefit rates
- Enrollee Notices will not be sent to enrollees assigned to this provider group
- The Plan will timely submit a Block Transfer filing prior to the termination of the contract between LaSalle Medical Associates and LPCH

**8. Alternate Hospital Outreach**
For each Alternate Hospital, confirm:
- Whether the Plan contacted the hospital regarding the Block Transfer
- Any concerns expressed by the hospital regarding its ability to accommodate redirected enrollees

**9. Services at Terminating Hospital Not Available at Alternate Hospitals**
Identify any outpatient or inpatient services available at LPCH but not at the Alternate Hospitals. If such services exist, explain where affected enrollees will receive them post-termination.

**10. Scheduled/Authorized Procedures Post-Termination**
Identify the number of enrollees who have a procedure scheduled and authorized by the Plan at the Terminating Hospital post-termination.
