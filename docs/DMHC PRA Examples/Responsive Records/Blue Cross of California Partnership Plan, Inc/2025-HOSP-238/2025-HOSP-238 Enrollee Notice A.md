# Enrollee Notice A — PCP Notice

_Audience: Enrollees whose primary care provider (PCP) admits to Lucile Salter Packard Children's Hospital. These members must choose a new PCP by August 15, 2025, or Anthem will assign one._

---

Dear Member:

Your primary care provider (PCP) admits to Lucile Salter Packard Children's Hospital. This hospital may be leaving Anthem's network on **September 1, 2025**. If you go to this hospital on or after that date and it's not an emergency, your visit may not be covered. Because your PCP cannot send you to an in-network hospital if you need care, you will need to choose another PCP by **August 15, 2025**.

**Alternate hospitals in Anthem's network near your home:**
- Eden Medical Center
- El Camino Health
- Kindred Hospital — San Francisco Bay Area
- Regional Medical of San Jose

**How to choose a new PCP:**
Call Customer Care toll free at 1-800-407-4627 (TTY 1-888-757-6034) or 1-888-285-7801 (TTY 711) for members in Los Angeles. Monday–Friday, 7 a.m.–7 p.m. Pacific time. If you don't choose a new PCP by September 1, 2025, Anthem will choose one for you and mail your new ID card no later than September 1, 2025.

**Continuity of Care — call Customer Care if any of the following apply:**
- You are pregnant and expect to deliver at Lucile Salter Packard Children's Hospital
- You are getting ongoing treatment there
- You have a child younger than 3 years old receiving care there
- You have scheduled surgery or a procedure there
- You have a maternal mental health condition there
- You have a terminal illness there
- You have a serious chronic condition there
- You have an acute condition

You may be able to keep your current doctors for up to 12 months as part of continuity of care. See your Member Handbook at www.anthem.com/ca/medi-cal (Benefits Tab → Member Materials).

Sincerely,
Anthem Blue Cross
