# Enrollee Notice C — Specialist / General Network Change Notice

_Audience: Enrollees whose specialist uses Lucile Salter Packard Children's Hospital. No PCP change required; enrollee may need to find a new specialist._

---

Dear Member:

As of **September 1, 2025**, the following hospital may no longer be in Anthem's network:

**Lucile Salter Packard Children's Hospital**

If you go to this hospital on or after that date and it's not an emergency, your visit may not be covered.

**Alternate hospitals in Anthem's network near your home:**
- Eden Medical Center
- El Camino Health
- Kindred Hospital — San Francisco Bay Area
- Regional Medical of San Jose

This change may mean you will need to see a different specialist. To pick a new specialist, call your primary care provider (PCP). Your Anthem benefits will not change. Please call us if you receive a bill from this hospital.

**Continuity of Care — call Customer Care toll free at 1-800-407-4627 (TTY 1-888-757-6034) or 1-888-285-7801 (TTY 711) for members in Los Angeles. Monday–Friday, 7 a.m.–7 p.m. Pacific time if any of the following apply:**
- You are pregnant and expect to deliver at Lucile Salter Packard Children's Hospital
- You are getting ongoing treatment there
- You have a child younger than 3 years old receiving care there
- You have scheduled surgery or a procedure there
- You have a maternal mental health condition there
- You have a terminal illness there
- You have a serious chronic condition there
- You have an acute condition there

You may be able to keep your current doctors for up to 12 months as part of continuity of care. See your Member Handbook at www.anthem.com/ca/medi-cal (Benefits Tab → Member Materials).

Sincerely,
Anthem Blue Cross
