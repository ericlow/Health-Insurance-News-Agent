# Notice of Termination — Stanford Health Care to Anthem Blue Cross

**Date:** February 25, 2025
**From:** Stanford Health Care / Lucile Salter Packard Children's Hospital at Stanford
**To:** Anthem Blue Cross, 21215 Burbank Blvd., CA9302-L02B, Woodland Hills, CA 91367

Stanford Health Care and Lucile Salter Packard Children's Hospital at Stanford, on behalf of themselves and the parties named below, have elected to terminate the following agreements with Blue Cross of California dba Anthem Blue Cross ("Anthem"), effective **12:01 AM Pacific Standard Time on September 1, 2025**:

| Agreement | Parties | Original Effective Date | Termination Section |
|---|---|---|---|
| SHC Facility Agreement | Anthem and Stanford Health Care dba Stanford Medical Center | September 8, 2014 | Section 8.1 |
| SHC Physician Group Agreement | Anthem and SHC Physician Group (incl. University Healthcare Alliance dba Stanford Medicine Partners) | January 1, 1995 | Section 12.2 |
| LPCH Facility Agreement | Anthem and Lucile Salter Packard Children's Hospital at Stanford | September 1, 2014 | Section 8.1 |
| LPCH Physician Group Agreement | Anthem and LPCH Physician Group (dba LPCH Medical Group) | January 1, 2006 | Section 12.1 |
| SHC-TV Facility Agreement | Anthem and Stanford Health Care Tri-Valley | June 1, 2013 | Section 8.2 |

"We look forward to starting discussions about a potential renewal of these agreements as soon as possible, and to concluding negotiations before the September 1, 2025 termination date."

**Signed:** Connie Wong, Vice President of Managed Care and Payor Relations, Stanford Health Care
