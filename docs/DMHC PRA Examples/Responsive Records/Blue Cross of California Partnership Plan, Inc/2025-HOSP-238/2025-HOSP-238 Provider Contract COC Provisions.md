# Anthem CA_UMXX_029 — Continuity of Care / Transition Assistance (CA)

**Document:** CA_UMXX_029
**Subject:** Continuity of Care/Transition Assistance — CA
**Division:** Government Business Division, Health Care Management
**Effective:** 03/30/2023 | **Last Revised:** 05/31/2023
**Applies to:** Medicaid/CHIP plans in California

---

## Policy

Anthem Medicaid is responsible for providing Access to Care, Continuity of Care (COC), and coordination of services for medically necessary medical, mental health, and durable medical equipment services.

This policy describes COC procedures for members transitioning from Medi-Cal Fee-For-Service (FFS) or another health plan to enroll as an Anthem member.

---

## Key Definitions

**Assessment:** Completed when a member has been seen in-person or via synchronous telehealth by a network provider who reviewed the member's condition and completed a new treatment plan.

**Continued Access to Care / Continuity of Care:** All members have the right to continue receiving Medi-Cal services covered under the MCP's contract, including mental health, even if they do not continue receiving services from their pre-existing provider. Authorizes continuation with a terminating/non-participating provider for up to 12 months from enrollment. The 12-month COC period can restart once if a member changes MCPs or loses and regains eligibility; it does not restart a second time.

**Durable Medical Equipment (DME):** Medically necessary equipment defined by 22 CCR section 51160.

**Medical Exemption Request (MER):** Temporary exemption from MCP enrollment until a member's condition stabilizes. Denied MERs are treated as automatic COC requests.

**Pre-existing relationship:** Member has seen an OON PCP, specialist, or select ancillary provider (PT/OT/ST/BHT/RT) for a non-emergency visit at least once in the 12 months prior to initial enrollment with Anthem.

---

## Procedure

### I. Continuity of Care Requirements

Anthem provides COC for members transitioning from Medi-Cal FFS or another health plan. COC available for up to 12 months after enrollment date if a pre-existing relationship exists.

### II. COC Provider Protections (HSC § 1373.96)

COC protections **extend to:**
- Primary Care Providers
- Specialists
- Select ancillary providers: PT, OT, RT, BHT, ST

COC protections do **not extend to:**
- Radiology, laboratory, dialysis centers
- Non-Emergency Medical Transportation (NEMT), Non-Medical Transportation (NMT)
- Other ancillary services (except with prior treatment authorization)
- Non-enrolled Medi-Cal providers
- Providers terminated for medical disciplinary cause, fraud, or criminal activity (CA B&P Code § 805(a)(6))

### III. Qualifying Conditions for COC

- **Acute condition:** Sudden onset, limited duration; COC for duration of acute condition
- **Serious chronic condition:** Persists or worsens over extended period; COC up to 12 months from termination date or coverage effective date
- **Pregnancy:** Through pregnancy and 12-month postpartum period; maternal mental health condition up to 12 months from diagnosis or end of pregnancy
- **Terminal illness:** For duration of illness, even if exceeding 12 months
- **Newborn child (birth–36 months):** Up to 12 months from termination or coverage effective date
- **Surgery or procedure authorized and scheduled within 180 days of contract termination or coverage effective date**

### IV. Processing COC Requests

Members, representatives, caregivers, providers, or terminating providers may contact Anthem to request continued Access to Care.

**A. Acceptance:** Anthem must accept COC requests by phone; cannot require written form if member prefers phone.

**B. Retroactive Requests:** Approved if: provider accepts Anthem/Medi-Cal FFS rates; services occurred after enrollment; service date within 30 calendar days of reimbursement request.

**C. Case Completion Timelines:**
- Non-urgent: 30 calendar days
- Urgent (upcoming appointments/pressing needs): 15 calendar days
- Urgent (identified risk of harm): as soon as possible, no longer than 3 calendar days

OON access allowed up to 12 months if: provider meets professional standards, is CA State Plan approved, supplies treatment information, and accepts Anthem/Medi-Cal FFS rates.

**D. Validating Pre-Existing Relationship:** Via FFS utilization data or claims data. Self-attestation alone is not sufficient.

**E. Member Notifications:**
1. **Acknowledgement:** Non-urgent within 7 calendar days; urgent within 3 calendar days. Must include date received and estimated resolution timeframe.
2. **Denial:** Includes decision, reason, and right to file grievance/appeal.
3. **Approval:** Includes decision, COC duration, transition process, and right to choose a network provider. MCP must notify member 30 days before end of COC period.
4. **Provider Notification (if COC approved):** Specifies authorized services, frequency/duration, reimbursement rate, billing instructions, UM/CM and Customer Care contact numbers.

**F. Scheduled Specialist Appointments:** Anthem allows transitioning members to keep authorized specialist appointments with OON providers during the 12-month COC period.

**G. Prior Treatment Authorizations:**
- Active prior treatment authorizations honored for 90 days following transition; remain in effect for duration or until new assessment, whichever is shorter.
- **DME:** Existing rentals and supplies maintained for minimum 90 days post-enrollment.
- **NEMT/NMT:** Members keep transportation modality under prior authorization until reassessment.

**H. Member and Provider Outreach:** Anthem translates COC information into threshold languages and provides call center training.

**K. Terminating Providers:**
- When an FFS provider or PMG/IPA terminates, members are notified and a transition plan is developed.
- Hospital termination: Anthem notifies members 60 days in advance only if the hospital is capitated and members are assigned to a specific hospital. For non-capitated terminations, Anthem notifies members if the hospital actually terminates.

**L. Transition Care Plan:**
- Only COC-approved patients may complete treatment with the terminating provider.
- UM/CM Nurse develops transition plan including: treating provider's active treatment plan; specific treatment goal before transfer; estimated safe transfer date.

**M. Covered California to Medi-Cal Transition:**
- Anthem contacts new members within 15 calendar days of enrollment.
- Honors active prior treatment authorizations for 90 days.
