# Enrollee Notice B — Recent Utilizer / Post-Termination Notice

_Audience: Enrollees whose records show they received or are scheduled to receive care at LPCH. No PCP change required._

Dear Member:

Our records show you received care or are scheduled to receive care at Lucile Salter Packard Children's Hospital. Starting **September 1, 2025**, this hospital is leaving Anthem's network. If you go to this hospital on or after that date and it's not an emergency, your visit may not be covered.

**Alternate hospitals in Anthem's network near your home:**
- Eden Medical Center
- El Camino Health
- Kindred Hospital — San Francisco Bay Area
- Regional Medical of San Jose

**Continuity of Care — call Customer Care toll free at 1-800-407-4627 (TTY 1-888-757-6034) or 1-888-285-7801 (TTY 711) for members in Los Angeles. Monday–Friday, 7 a.m.–7 p.m. Pacific time if any of the following apply:**
- You are pregnant and expect to deliver at the terminating hospital
- You are getting ongoing treatment there
- You have a child younger than 3 years old receiving care there
- You have scheduled surgery or a procedure there
- You have a maternal mental health condition
- You have a terminal illness there
- You have a serious chronic condition there
- You have an acute condition

You may be able to keep your current doctors for up to 12 months as part of continuity of care. See your Member Handbook at www.anthem.com/ca/medi-cal.
