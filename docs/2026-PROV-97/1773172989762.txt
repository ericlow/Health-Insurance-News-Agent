What if I am receiving ongoing treatment on July 1, 2026 or have been

You may be eligible for Continuity of Care. This process allows you to continue in-
network coverage for certain medical and behavioral treatment for a defined period
of time after the provider leaves your network, based on your plan benefits, federal
law or state law. To be eligible, you must be receiving a course of ongoing,
medically necessary treatment. Here’s what you need to know:
Qualifying conditions and treatments
Your plan materials include details about the conditions and treatments that qualify
for continuity of care. Depending on your plan benefits, federal law or state laws,
such conditions may include but, are not limited to:
• upcoming surgery,
• treatment of a serious chronic or acute condition,
• terminal illness,
• care of a newborn child between birth and age 36 months,
• care during pregnancy,
• maternal mental health, or
• any other procedure that is authorized by the plan as part of a documented
course of treatment.

y tế/nha khoa theo số 1-800-244-6224, hoặc gọi số 1-866-421-8629 để biết thông tin về chương trình chăm sóc sức khỏe tâm
